### Native primitives

- Links are currently authored as native anchors or Next.js `Link` depending on the route.
- The only rendered native buttons are the previous/next controls inside a multi-image `ProjectCard`.
- There are currently no reusable input, textarea, select, table, modal, or dialog primitives.
- Form controls and buttons inherit the application font through `button, input, textarea, select { font: inherit; }`.

## Repeated patterns and consolidation candidates

These are suggestions for later review. Nothing listed here should be deleted or merged without an explicit task.

1. The profile rail markup is duplicated in `app/page.tsx` and `app/work-experience/page.tsx`. A future `ProfileRail` component could accept the home-link behavior as a prop.
2. The sticky panel header and LinkedIn contact treatment appear on both routes. A future `PanelHeader` could accept route-specific navigation children.
3. Email, social, and resume link markup is repeated across both profile rails. It should be consolidated with the rail rather than split into unrelated abstractions.
4. `PillButton` and `ProjectTag` overlap as pill-like link primitives, but their content and accessibility behavior differ. Compare requirements before merging.
5. `Footer` repeats social and email destinations already shown in the profile rail and is currently unused.
6. `ProofCard` and `StoryStep` are unused card-like components. Keep them until their intended sections are confirmed.
7. `app/heading.module.css` contains unused legacy profile/social styles that overlap the active global styles in `app/globals.css`.
8. `public/icons/d.svg`, `da.svg`, `ja.svg`, and `s.svg` are unused placeholder-style assets that overlap current React, TypeScript, JavaScript, and Next.js skill concepts. Review before removing; do not assume they are disposable.

## Typography

Use the Sans Seierf font 
- Avoid component-level `font-family` declarations unless a deliberately different typographic role is introduced.

### Text inside image assets

Page CSS cannot restyle text embedded inside an SVG or raster image loaded through `next/image`.

- Some small SVG assets contain literal SVG `<text>` elements with their own font declarations, including the active Expo icon and unused placeholder icons such as `d.svg`, `da.svg`, `ja.svg`, and `s.svg`.
- Several larger skill and resume assets render lettering as vector paths or embedded image artwork. That lettering is fixed artwork rather than DOM text.
- These assets should not be mass-rewritten merely to enforce the page font. Replace or deliberately regenerate an individual asset only when its visual design is being revised.
- Accessible names must continue to come from the surrounding `Image` `alt` text or labeled interactive element, not from assuming embedded artwork is readable to assistive technology.

## Asset guidance

- Active local assets live under `public/icons`, `public/images`, and `public/fonts`.
- Technology image dimensions belong with their entries in `app/content/proof.ts` so `next/image` can preserve aspect ratio and layout stability.
- Do not infer that an unreferenced asset is safe to delete. Confirm its design intent and version history first.
- Preserve meaningful `alt` text for content images; use empty alt text only for decorative icons already labeled by adjacent text.
